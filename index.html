Reading gym

15-minute practice with a real book

Chunking drill

Use a paragraph from your own book. Read naturally, grouping words into meaningful phrases. Then summarise each sentence in your own words.

Try this example

Although the experiment appeared simple, the researcher carefully controlled the temperature, measured the results repeatedly, and compared each observation before reaching a conclusion.

Possible chunks: “Although the experiment appeared simple” / “the researcher carefully controlled the temperature” / “measured the results repeatedly” / “and compared each observation” / “before reaching a conclusion.”

  1. Read a paragraph for 3 minutes.
  2. Notice phrase groups instead of staring at each word.
  3. Pause after each sentence and capture its main idea.
  4. Close the book and explain the paragraph from memory.
  5. Check what you missed. Prioritise comprehension over speed.
